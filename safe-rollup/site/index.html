Frequently asked questions

How is Safe Rollup different from a Stage 0 rollup?

For users, it isn't. In both, the operator's multisig has the final say over user funds. Safe Rollup is the same arrangement without the code in between.

What are Zero Proofs™?

A proof system with no prover and no verifier: a batch is accepted when it is posted. It is also what any rollup's proof system becomes once an admin can replace the verifier without delay.

Why was my withdrawal vetoed?

The reason is onchain, in the Safe transaction that vetoed it, as final as the veto itself. The compliance policy reviews every valid withdrawal and vetoes about one in three, for reasons such as unusual activity (the account attempted a withdrawal), source of funds (the assets originated on Ethereum) and proof of reserves (the payout would reduce reserves).

Can I force a withdrawal if the operator censors me?

No. On a Stage 0 rollup you can, unless the admin upgrades the bridge first.

Who can see my transactions?

Whoever the operator allows. Today that is anyone using our RPC or our explorer. The batch data behind them stays with the operator.

Is it safe?

Safe as in Safe multisig. Your assets sit in a Safe, and its owners can veto any payout. Whether that makes your funds safe depends on who holds the keys, which is also true of every Stage 0 rollup.

Can I get the assurance report?

It is available on request.

Why does this exist?

To show how much of a rollup stack exists to constrain the operator, and how little remains when the operator is not constrained. The L1 side became a Safe and 199 lines of code. The OP Stack fork went from 633,212 lines to 72,562.

Is this a real product?

No. This is a parody. It runs on Sepolia with test ETH and is not intended for any other use.

Is the Safe Ecosystem Foundation involved?

No. This project is not affiliated with, sponsored or endorsed by the Safe Ecosystem Foundation. It uses the unmodified Safe contracts because they are good at being a multisig.
